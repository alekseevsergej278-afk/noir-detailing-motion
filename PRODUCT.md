# NOIR Detailing Motion
## Platform
Responsive web landing page, Russian language.
## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with a bundled React, TypeScript, Tailwind and Motion gallery.
## Product
Independent premium demonstration for car detailing studios. Visitors explore services, work and the studio approach, then choose a contact channel. Buyers of the demo are small-business owners.
## Constraints
No forms, tracking, invented reviews or business statistics. The requested gallery uses CSS perspective. Demonstration prices and contact data must be labelled. Eight services, studio, benefits, gallery, process and contact sections. Reduced motion support and responsive layouts from 320px.
## Brand commitments
NOIR Detailing Motion. Dark automotive editorial, warm metallic accent, cinematic car photography, generous typography and purposeful motion.
